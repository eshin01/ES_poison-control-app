Coding corrections alone were worth about $20,845 on the uninsured bills. The larger gains came from entitlements the patient did not know existed: free care under the hospital’s own policy, the good faith estimate, the No Surprises Act.

“Patient alone” assumes a 40% chance of asking for a discount and getting 15%, and a 30% chance of spotting the duplicate CT. Both are assumptions, chosen to be generous to the patient.
