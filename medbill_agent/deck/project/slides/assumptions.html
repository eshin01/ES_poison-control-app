Assumptions behind the simulation, and what is not yet verified

Hospital model

  • Accepts 95% of rule-text findings, 70% of record-dependent ones, 25% of speculative ones
  • Removes half of an uncoded miscellaneous charge when pressed
  • Self-pay discount 30%; lump-sum settlement 45 to 55% off corrected charges
  • Nonprofit assistance: free to 200% of poverty, sliding tiers to 400%, charges capped at amounts generally billed
  • Settles at the good faith estimate 80% of the time once a dispute is filed
  • Insured hardship reduction of 50% on cost sharing when the policy covers insured patients

Still to verify against primary text

  • The 120-day and 240-day periods in 26 CFR 1.501(r)-6
  • The current No Surprises Act dispute filing fee
  • 2026 federal poverty guidelines (2025 values shipped)
  • State protections marked “verify” in the data file
  • Illustrative Medicare rates; replace with current CMS files
  • Market figures are self-reported by vendors

Every assumption is a named constant in one dictionary. Change it and the four scenarios rerun in under two seconds. The pilot’s job is to replace these constants with observed rates.

End here, not on a send-off. The honest version of this pitch is that the mechanism is built and the behavioral constants need real bills to calibrate.
